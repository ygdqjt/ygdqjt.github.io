About Us

Leading Manufacturer of Electrical Equipment

Jiangsu Yanggang Electrical Equipment Co., Ltd. is a professional manufacturer specializing in cable tray systems and electrical conduit solutions. With years of experience in the industry, we are committed to providing high-quality products and excellent service to customers worldwide.

Our state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities and rigorous quality control processes ensure that every product meets international standards. We serve customers in construction, industrial, and infrastructure projects across the globe.

Our mission is to deliver reliable, durable, and cost-effective electrical solutions that meet the evolving needs of our clients. We pride ourselves on our technical expertise, competitive pricing, and commitment to customer satisfaction.
