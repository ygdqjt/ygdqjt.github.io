About Jiangsu Yanggang

Our Story

Who We Are

Jiangsu Yanggang Electrical Equipment Co., Ltd. is a high-tech enterprise that integrates scientific research, production, sales, and service. Founded in 2015 in Yangzhong City, Jiangsu Province, we specialize in manufacturing electrical support and distribution equipment serving industrial, commercial, and infrastructure projects worldwide.

Our product portfolio includes cable tray series, low-voltage busbar trunking series, conduit series, high and low voltage switchgear series, high-voltage busbar trunking series, and seismic support series — providing one-stop solutions for modern electrical installations.

🏭 Located in Dongjiu Industrial Park, Yangzhong City, Jiangsu Province, China

Our Mission & Values

Adhering to the business philosophy of "Building a Century-Old Enterprise, Pursuing Supreme Service", we uphold the corporate ambition of "Carrying Goods with Integrity, Promoting Industry, Serving the Country".

Our pragmatic, innovative, and efficient corporate spirit has attracted a large number of technical talents. We actively introduce foreign enterprise management models and adhere to strict product quality control — products are widely used in chemical industry, metallurgical power, mines, hospitals, and housing construction.

We provide timely and punctual products and services, sincerely cooperating with design institutes and customers to create a win-win situation for the prosperity and development of China's electrical industry.
