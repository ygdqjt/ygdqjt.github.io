JDG / EMT Conduit Series

Galvanized steel tubular pathway for electrical wiring

JDG Conduit & EMT Conduit Pipes

JDG conduits (Chinese standard, similar to EMT) offer 20-30% cost advantage over IMC/RMC with excellent corrosion resistance. Hot-dip galvanized coating (80-120g/m² zinc).

  • JDG Conduit — Nominal 1/2" to 2", 3m length, .8-13/pc
  • EMT Conduit — US standard, easy installation
  • Accessories: Couplings, connectors, elbows, junction boxes
  • Compliant with GB/T 17194, UL 797, IEC 61386
  • Fire-rated and outdoor-rated options available
