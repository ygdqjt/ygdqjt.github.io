🏭 Safety Standards for Cable Tray Manufacturers

📅 November 18, 2025 · ⏱️ 10 min read

The safety standards of cable tray manufacturers cover multiple aspects to ensure product quality and safety. Here's a comprehensive overview of the main safety standards that govern the cable tray industry.

1. Material Standards

Cable tray materials must comply with relevant national and international standards with sufficient strength and corrosion resistance:

  • Galvanized Steel — Hot-dip or electro-galvanized, zinc coating typically 60-120g/m²
  • Stainless Steel — Grades 304 or 316 for corrosive environments
  • Aluminum — Lightweight, naturally corrosion-resistant
  • FRP (Fiberglass Reinforced Plastic) — Non-conductive, chemical-resistant

2. Structural Standards

The structure of cable trays must be reasonably designed to meet support and protection requirements:

ParameterRequirement
Load CapacityMust withstand dead load + 50% live load (per NEMA VE 1)
SpanMaximum span specified per load class (typically 1.5-6m)
Fire ResistanceTested per ASTM E84 or equivalent
Finish IntegrityNo peeling, flaking or delamination

3. Installation Standards

Cable tray installation must comply with relevant national standards. Key requirements:

  • Maintain levelness and verticality — max deviation 1/500 of length
  • Follow NEC Article 392 (USA) or IEC 61537 (International)
  • Proper support spacing — every 1.5-2.5m depending on load class
  • Minimum 2× cable diameter bend radius for all cable runs
  • Continuous grounding of all metal tray sections
💡 Pro Tip: Always verify local electrical codes before installation — NEC Article 392 governs cable tray installations in the United States, while IEC 61537 applies internationally. China's GB 50217 standard covers cable tray design in mainland China.

4. Fire Prevention Standards

NEMA VE 1 — USA

Establishes structural integrity, safety factors, and fire test requirements for cable tray systems.

UL 568 — Fire Test

Standard for fire test of cable tray materials — evaluates flame spread, smoke development, and fuel contribution.

IEC 61537 — International

International standard specifying requirements for cable management systems including tray, ladder and related supports.

5. Environmental Standards

  • ISO 14001 — Environmental management system certification
  • RoHS Compliance — Restriction of hazardous substances in materials
  • REACH Compliance — EU regulation on chemical substances
  • Recyclability — Steel and aluminum trays are 100% recyclable
