🎯 What is JDG Conduit Pipe?

JDG conduit (also called electrical conduit, EMT conduit, or galvanized steel conduit) is a tubular pathway used to protect and route electrical wiring in buildings.

JDG conduits are widely used in commercial buildings, industrial facilities, and residential wiring to provide mechanical protection, electromagnetic shielding, and fire resistance for electrical cables.

💡 Pro Tip: JDG conduit is the Chinese standard (similar to EMT in USA). It's 20-30% cheaper than IMC/RMC but offers excellent corrosion resistance with hot-dip galvanization!

🔧 Installation Guide (Step-by-Step)

Step 1: Planning & Layout

  • Determine conduit route (shortest path with minimal bends)
  • Calculate number of bends (max 360° per run)
  • Select proper conduit size (fill capacity ≤ 40% for >2 cables)

Step 2: Cutting & Threading

  • Use a pipe cutter (not hacksaw) for clean cuts
  • Ream inside edge to remove burrs
  • Thread ends with proper die (taper thread per GB/T 17194)

Step 3: Bending

  • Use a conduit bender (not heat bending!)
  • Maintain minimum bend radius (6x conduit diameter)
  • Mark bend angle on floor for accuracy

Step 4: Assembly & Support

  • Connect sections with couplings (threaded or set-screw type)
  • Secure with clamps every 1.0-1.5 meters
  • Ground all metal conduits continuously

Step 5: Wire Pulling

  • Install string line first
  • Pull wires with lubricant (not soap!)
  • Never pull more than 3 cables through one conduit
